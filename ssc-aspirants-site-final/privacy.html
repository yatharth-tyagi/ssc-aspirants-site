Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SSCAspirants_Official (“we”, “us”) runs the website sscaspirants-official.in, which offers notes, mock tests and previous year papers for SSC exams. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form – nobody, including us, can read it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google profile ID from Google.
  • Profile photo: only if you upload one.
  • Your study activity: tests you take, your answers, time spent, scores, ranks, saved questions, chapters opened or marked complete, and daily quiz results.
  • Rank calculator: the marks, category, exam date and shift you calculate, so we can show ranks. We do not keep your answer-key link, roll number or the name on it.
  • Reports you send: when you report a mistake in a question.
  • Technical data: a login cookie that keeps you signed in, and settings stored on your device (light/dark mode, text size, unfinished tests).

2. Why we use it

  • To create and protect your account and keep you logged in.
  • To show your results, ranks, analysis, saved questions and reading progress.
  • To make leaderboards. Leaderboards show your name and score to other students – never your email.
  • To send you a password-reset code when you ask for one.
  • To fix mistakes and improve our notes and tests.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Who handles your data for us

  • Cloudflare – hosts the website and stores accounts and results.
  • Resend – sends password-reset emails.
  • Google – only if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • YouTube (Google) – videos inside notes are played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the video.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and study data while your account is active. When a new test series starts we may clear old results. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. You can change your name, photo and password yourself in Account details. For anything else, write to us at (email). We reply within 30 days.

6. Children

This website is meant for SSC exam aspirants aged 18 or above. If you are under 18, please use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

7. Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, hashed passwords and login sessions that expire. No system is perfectly safe, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you on the website.
